# CRO by objections (O-CO)

People don't fail to convert because your button is the wrong color. They fail because a
**specific doubt** went unanswered at the moment of decision. Conversion optimization = surfacing
those objections and answering each with evidence, in the right place.

## The core idea

For every ask (sign up, pay, start trial), the visitor is silently running objections:
*"Is this for someone like me? Will it actually work? Is it worth the price? What if I'm wrong?
What happens after I click?"* Your job is to **list the objections** and pair each with a
**counter-objection (CO)** — an asset that removes the doubt.

Research beats best practices. A "best practice" is the average answer to someone else's
objections. Find *your* visitors' objections.

## Build the O-CO table

| Objection (what they doubt) | Counter-objection (the asset that answers it) | Where it belongs |
|---|---|---|
| "Is this for me?" | Specific audience callout, use-case, a customer who looks like them | Hero / above the fold |
| "Does it actually work?" | Proof: demo, before/after, data, a concrete result | Near the claim |
| "Why should I trust you?" | Social proof, logos, reviews, security/compliance | Next to the ask |
| "Is it worth the price?" | Value framing, ROI, comparison, what they lose by not acting | Pricing |
| "What if it's the wrong choice?" | Risk reversal: guarantee, free trial, easy cancel, no card | At every CTA |
| "What happens after I click?" | Set expectations: "3 steps, 2 minutes, no card" | On/next to the button |

The strongest CO is **evidence** (a real result, a real customer), then **risk reversal**, then
**framing**. Decoration (nicer hero image) rarely moves a real objection.

## How to find the objections (research first)

- **Sales/support logs & chat transcripts** — the literal words of hesitation.
- **Cancellation & lost-deal reasons** — objections that already won.
- **Session recordings / heatmaps** — where they hesitate, re-read, or bail.
- **A 1-question exit or on-page survey** — "what almost stopped you from signing up?"
- **Review mining** — yours and competitors': the phrases people use for doubt and delight.
Use the customers' *own language* in the counter-objection. Matching their words converts.

## Prioritize and test

1. Rank objections by **how many** hit them × **how close to the money** they occur (a checkout
   doubt outranks a blog doubt).
2. For the top objection, add or strengthen the counter-objection asset.
3. Frame each test as a hypothesis: *"Visitors doubt [X]; adding [CO] will lift [metric] because
   it removes that doubt."* — not "let's try a bigger button."
4. Measure the specific step, not just top-line conversion.

## How to use this skill

1. Pick one page/flow and one primary conversion goal.
2. List the top 5 objections a real visitor has *there* (from research, not guessing).
3. Write the counter-objection for each and place it where the doubt occurs.
4. Ship the highest-impact CO as a hypothesis-driven test; read the result at that step.

Pairs with `jobs-to-be-done` (the anxieties/forces map straight onto objections) and `hook-model`
(convert the first action, then build the loop).
